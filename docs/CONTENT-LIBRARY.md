## Reading pathways

- Exploring: The good news: trusting Jesus → Meet Jesus in Mark → First steps in faith.
- Beginning: First steps in faith → Learning to pray honestly → Belonging, baptism, and church life → Read the Bible with understanding.
- Returning: Begin again with God → Rhythms of grace → a Bible-book reading plan.
- Growing: Read the Bible with understanding → James: faith in everyday action → Called to serve.
- Children and youth: start with the age-appropriate personal plan; use adult-guided or group readings for shared learning.

These are editorial recommendations, not an implemented automatic recommendation engine.

## Remaining expansion opportunities

This foundation supplies entry points rather than every possible audience/topic combination. Future depth can include teen boys’ and girls’ collections, singleness, caring for aging relatives, retirement transitions, sustained Gospel/book studies, seasonal readings, and more advanced group courses. Preschool content would need a separate adult-led format. Avoid duplicating shared Christian teaching just to fill every demographic cell.

## Review status
